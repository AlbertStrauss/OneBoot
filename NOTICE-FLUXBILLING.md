# FluxBilling source attribution

OneBoot v1.3's Ubuntu live helpers are adapted from
https://github.com/FluxBilling-app/FluxBilling-OS-Installer
at commit `594deb47a8d6e73d6597168733248fec4f572608`.

The original files and GNU GPL version 2 license are retained under
`vendor/fluxbilling/`. Adapted files are generated by
`scripts/prepare-oneboot-v1.3-autoinstall.py`; they remain licensed under
GPL-2.0-or-later. OneBoot is a separate project, with no implication of
endorsement by the upstream authors.

Adaptations include a single-disk-only selector, private RAM credential
transport, DNS from the operator's network form, fixed-version mirror URLs,
OneBoot diagnostics/branding and the Manual/Auto workflow. The upstream BIOS
USB keyboard policy and HP iLO4 EFI keyboard patch are retained for the new
loader build. Physical qualification of the upstream ISO does not establish
qualification of this modified ISO.
